> TL;DR: The [Technological Singularity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technological_singularity) has a profound impact on our present and near future. Yet, human nature remains: Curiosity drives value creation, paving the way to the imagined destination—the path to a fulfilled life. Mastering value creation turns real-world challenges into real-life changes.

**_The Technological Singularity_** is a probable future where technological growth becomes exponentially rapid and unstoppable, surpassing human intelligence.

It isn't a fixed point—but more like the creation of Kaiju in Pacific Rim—always coming faster and bigger.

And We're in the thick of it right now.

## You’ve Stepped into Technological Singularity

Just like the iPhone, [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/) is sounding the clarion call for another wave of innovation.

Various AI applications are causing a stir.

From text, image, to video, AI-generated information is everywhere.

And, following [the 10 stages of AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tFx_UNW9I1U), this is just the beginning.

Now, AI simulates human-like reasoning and decision-making processes.

Soon, they'd:

- Possess human-like intelligence and cognitive abilities.
- Attain a level of consciousness and self-awareness, comprehending their existence and relationship to the external world.
- Surpass human intelligence, solving problems beyond human comprehension.
- Chart their own evolutionary path, fostering self-development and collective intelligence.
- Grasp and interact with the broader universe, potentially on a cosmic scale.
- Mirror the capabilities of a god.

This unstoppable series of advancements has already left its mark.

### _Current Impact:_ [Unprecedented Wealth Inequality](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/24945487-humans-need-not-apply) (The Gap between New Rich and Poor)

In the dance of progress, ownership of assets remains a key player, accelerating the pace for some more than others.

The greater your resources today, the swifter and earlier you can tap into emerging technologies, using them as a catalyst for further gains.

These newfound advantages wield unprecedented power, armed with exponential potential.

Should the status quo persist, [the 99/1 Rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1%25_rule) could morph into an even more staggering 999.../1 Rule.

This is not just a wealth gap; it's a chasm of opportunities, and it's time to confront the imminent challenges on the horizon.

### _Near Future:_ What If AI and High-Tech Solve All Problems, Surpass Humans, and Revolutionize the World

If this holds true, it would unfold after the stage of [Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superintelligence)—a realm where problem-solving skills surpass the sum of all human intelligence.

Yet, even if AI were to outstrip human capabilities, all problems would never be solved.

**The expansion of the problem outpaces its solution**—the entire [universe continues its expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expansion_of_the_universe), and the pace quickens.

**Learn more; discover we know less.**

**The unknown always outpaces its understanding**—this holds not only for humans but for all forms of intelligence.

The future might witness a collaborative venture where humans work hand in hand with AI to unravel the mysteries of the universe (or the [multiverse](https://www.britannica.com/science/multiverse)).

## Value Creation: The Everlasting Skill to Live Fulfilled

In the Technological Singularity, AI stands as our ally. However, just knowing how to work with it is not enough—what truly matters is the ability to spot value.

In the era of [gold rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Gold_Rush), it's useless just knowing how to use a shovel; rather, it's the wisdom to discover the gold.

> [“Technology changes, but people stay the same.”](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/454121-technology-changes-but-people-stay-the-same) ― [Daniel H. Wilson](https://www.danielhwilson.com/), [Robopocalypse](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/9634967-robopocalypse)

Even as technology grows exponentially, these truths remain:

- **Curiosity is fuel.**
- **Value creation is the vessel.**
- **Imagination is the destination.**

Curiosity drives value creation, paving the way to the imagined destination—the path to a fulfilled life.

**Value creation** has stood as a cornerstone in every revolution throughout history.

### The Constant Amidst [Revolutions](https://www.ynharari.com/book/sapiens-2/)

- In the [_Cognitive Revolution_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_revolution): **Story** creates value for **Connection**.
- In the [_Agricultural Revolution_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_revolution): **Farming** creates value for **Safety**.
- In the [_Scientific Revolution_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_Revolution): **Understanding** creates value for **Strength**.
- In the [_Industrial Revolution_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Revolution): **Automation** creates value for **Efficiency**.
- In the [_Digital Revolution_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Revolution): **Empowering** creates value for **Productivity**.
- In the _Singularity Revolution_: **Growth** creates value for **Possibility**.

These elements will foster the creation of value for **more value creation**.

We're in a movement of **value creation.**

### The CIA Momentum of Value Creation

_What's the best practice of value creation?_

- **Craft:** The source of value creation—synthesizing, problem-solving, building.
- **Influence:** The vessel of value creation—storytelling, distributing, selling.
- **Automate:** The autopilot of value creation—simplifying, streamlining, systemizing.

These 3 actions form the cornerstone of value creation.

Craft without influence = nothing.

Influence without craft = nothing.

Craft + Influence without automation = limited.

**Mastering these 3 skillsets makes you unstoppable.**
