## Overview

`klkuo.guru` is the public personal brand website of KL KUO (郭耿綸), hosting mature products, service information, essays, and case studies. Browsing this site does not require an account or user-submitted profile data.

When you choose to submit an application form, book a call, join a waitlist, or enroll in a paid program (such as IntenTutor, GatheRead, or Life Cogamer), this policy explains how your data is handled under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). I **never sell** your personal data.

## Technical Data and Web Analytics

- **Hosting:** This site is currently deployed on **GitHub Pages** and is planned to migrate to **Vercel** later. Hosting and content delivery infrastructure may process technical request metadata (such as IP address, requested URL, referrer, browser metadata, and timestamps) to serve the site reliably and securely.
- **Analytics:** This site uses **Umami** to understand aggregate traffic and page usage patterns without cross-site advertising tracking.

## Browser-Side Storage

The site stores your theme preference in browser local storage so light and dark mode choices persist on your device.

## Third-Party Services Used Across the Business

Depending on how you interact with my services, the following third-party providers may process your data (including on servers located outside Taiwan):

- **Application & feedback forms:** **Formbricks** (hosted in Frankfurt, Germany).
- **Scheduling & live sessions:** **Cal.com**, **Google Calendar**, and **Google Meet**.
- **Pages, lead magnets & internal records:** **Notion** (Notion Site for testing landing pages, terms pages, and lead magnets; Notion databases for internal CRM and operational trackers).
- **Waitlists & email sequences:** **Loops** — used only when you explicitly opt in to a waitlist, lead magnet, or newsletter (every email includes a free one-click unsubscribe; applying for a service never auto-subscribes you to marketing).
- **Social & publishing platforms:** **X, Threads, LinkedIn, Facebook, Medium, and Vocus** — used for public engagement and content. Visiting those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies; private applicant or client data is never uploaded to social platforms.

## Your Rights and Contact

Under Article 3 of Taiwan's PDPA, you may contact me at any time to access, review, obtain a copy of, correct, stop processing, or request deletion of your personal data (subject to statutory accounting retention requirements). Each paid service also provides its own dedicated terms page or written consent form detailing retention and deletion schedules.
